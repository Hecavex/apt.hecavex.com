## Analytic scope

UNC1151 is an intrusion cluster, not the complete Ghostwriter influence apparatus. Its technical activity can acquire accounts, documents and communications that later support public narratives or hack-and-leak operations. The people producing and amplifying that content may extend beyond the operators represented here.

## Identity-focused operations

The 2026 Polish campaign targeted credentials and two-factor authentication codes. This matters defensively because the objective is a valid session or account, not simply a malicious-file execution. Detection and response must include identity telemetry, mailbox changes and suspicious session behaviour.

## European relevance

Public reporting documents operations against Poland, Lithuania, Latvia, Germany and Ukraine. The target set concentrates on people and organisations able to shape democratic, diplomatic or security decisions. Individual phishing waves remain source-scoped rather than being converted into a claim of continuous access across every country.

## Attribution limitations

The Belarus link is a strong published assessment, but public attribution language is not perfectly uniform. The dossier retains who made each claim and at what confidence. Shared targets, messaging and infrastructure are not sufficient by themselves to combine Belarusian and Russian services into a single operator record.
