The United States alleges that the APT31 programme operated since at least 2010 in support of the Hubei State Security Department. Its described access path began with highly targeted emails containing hidden tracking links, used the resulting device and network information to support further compromise, and in some cases produced long-lived access to email and cloud data.

The 2021 reconnaissance against UK parliamentarians and the campaign against an unclassified Czech Ministry of Foreign Affairs network since at least 2022 are recorded as government-attributed examples of the wider targeting pattern. They are not treated as proof that every event shared the same operator, infrastructure or procedure.
