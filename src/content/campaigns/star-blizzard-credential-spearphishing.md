This record is a descriptive campaign umbrella for the recurring credential-theft procedure in the selected sources; it is not a formal campaign name assigned by a publisher.

The NCSC and partners describe researched impersonation, rapport-building, malicious links or linked documents, Evilginx-assisted capture of credentials and session material, mailbox access, forwarding-rule creation and follow-on targeting. DOJ separately alleges a Callisto conspiracy operating from at least October 2016 through October 2022. Those allegations are preserved as allegations and do not define every later Star Blizzard observation.

Microsoft's January 2025 report states that more than 30 civil-society entities were targeted between January 2023 and August 2024. That bounded observation does not establish continuous activity on every date within the range.
