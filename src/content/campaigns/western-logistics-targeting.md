The campaign combined credential attacks, spearphishing, vulnerability exploitation and post-compromise email collection. Target selection extended through supplier and trust relationships rather than stopping at the organisation directly coordinating assistance.

Related reconnaissance targeted IP cameras near border crossings, military installations and railway stations, providing a potential view of physical materiel movements into Ukraine.
