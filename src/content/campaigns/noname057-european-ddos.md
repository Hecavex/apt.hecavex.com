This campaign groups the recurring, politically timed availability attacks that official sources attribute to NoName057(16). It is an analytic container for the demonstrated operating model rather than a list of every target advertised in actor channels.

Operation Eastwood documented the participant network, automated DDoS tooling and distributed infrastructure behind the activity. The multinational advisory attributes DDoSia development, infrastructure funding and target selection to CISM personnel. CERT-EU records a launched campaign against Lithuanian airports and, at a lower evidence level, a separate claim against Latvia's National Defence Academy. Later CERT-EU reporting indicates that claims and bounded effects continued after the July 2025 disruption. Individual target claims remain unconfirmed unless supported by victim or authoritative third-party evidence.
