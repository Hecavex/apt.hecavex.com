Microsoft observed the campaign in mid-November 2024 and said it appeared to have terminated by the end of that month. The actor impersonated a US government official, used a deliberately broken QR code to encourage a reply, then supplied a shortened link to a page containing a WhatsApp linked-device QR code.

Successful scanning could expose messages through the linked account. The record does not classify the QR code as malware, does not infer compromise from delivery alone and does not describe the campaign as currently active.
