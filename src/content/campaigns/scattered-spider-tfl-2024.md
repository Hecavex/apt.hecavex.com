The NCA attributed the TfL intrusion to leading members of Scattered Spider and documented disabled systems, operational disruption and financial loss. The wider advisory explains the collective's reliance on social engineering and identity compromise.

This campaign does not attribute separate 2025 UK retail incidents to Scattered Spider; the NCSC described that identification as media speculation.
