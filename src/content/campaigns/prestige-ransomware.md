Microsoft assessed in November 2022 that IRIDIUM, now Seashell Blizzard, very likely executed the attacks. The observed deployment methods included remote execution, scheduled tasks and distribution through Active Directory Group Policy.

The ransomware presentation should not be treated as proof of a financially motivated operation; the campaign's context and targeting support a disruptive interpretation.
