The campaign represents the shared service, infrastructure and operating playbook, not a single intrusion crew. Affiliates selected and accessed victims while administrators supplied ransomware, leak infrastructure and payment services.

Operation Cronos materially degraded the ecosystem and exposed its administration. The campaign remains open-ended because law-enforcement action did not establish permanent cessation or ownership of every later LockBit-branded incident.
