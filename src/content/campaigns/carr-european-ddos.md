US and EU authorities describe CARR as conducting sustained DDoS activity from 2022 against Ukraine and organisations in countries supporting Ukraine. A multinational advisory assesses that GRU Unit 74455 likely funded the group's DDoS tooling through at least September 2024. The public assessment is stronger for the recurring campaign and target categories than for every individual target announced by the actor.

The activity is generally described as low impact and technically unsophisticated. Those qualifiers describe observed effect and method; they do not turn an attempted availability attack into a benign event.
