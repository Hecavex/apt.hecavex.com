CERT Polska documented a targeted credential campaign against people active in Polish public life. The phishing flow attempted to collect both passwords and two-factor codes, making the campaign an identity-access operation rather than a generic influence label.

The record does not assign all Ghostwriter information operations to UNC1151. It covers the technical intrusion component supported by the cited evidence.
