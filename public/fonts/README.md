# Self-hosted fonts

HECAVEX APT serves its typography locally and does not contact a font CDN.

The WOFF2 files in this directory come from Fontsource 5.3.0 packages for Inter and IBM Plex Mono. The site uses Latin and Latin Extended subsets: Inter normal weights 400–700 plus 400 italic, and IBM Plex Mono normal weights 400–700.

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their bundled licence texts are `INTER-OFL.txt` and `IBM-PLEX-MONO-OFL.txt`.

When updating these assets, retain the subset and weight naming convention, update the Fontsource version recorded here, and keep the corresponding OFL texts with the binaries.
